Ask Mail redesign

Three directions for one shared SwiftUI component on iOS and macOS.

Pick a direction

Each page shows the same markup rendered in a Mac window and on an iPhone, with Empty, Working and Answer states and light and dark modes. All three use the question from the current screenshot, "Group my unread emails, I want to clean some of them", so you can compare them directly.

A

Result blocks

The familiar chat, but answers are structured, actionable blocks instead of paragraphs.

  • Groups with counts, expandable previews and per-group buttons
  • "Searched: Unread · Inbox · 2 accounts" line you can edit
  • Visible working steps, Undo toast
Smallest change from today. Mac: inspector column.
B

Query lens

No chat. The question becomes editable filter chips over the mail list, and the answer is the grouped list itself.

  • Multi-select sections with a floating action bar
  • Refinements replace the lens, with a breadcrumb back
  • Wide layout adds a summary and shortcuts panel
Best for finding and cleaning up mail. Mac: takes over the list area.
C

Briefing

Reads like a short, well-sourced memo. The question is the headline and each claim links to its email.

  • Inline sender chips open a peek with the highlighted sentence
  • @ scopes: Unread, a person, a thread, a date
  • Suggested actions you confirm
Best for questions and catching up. Mac: inspector column.

What all three fix from today's screen

  • Wrong answer shape. "Group my unread" was treated as a text search for the word "unread". All three turn it into structured filters (unread, folder, account) before the model writes anything.
  • No way to act. Cleaning up needs Mark read, Archive and Unsubscribe right in the answer, with Undo.
  • Unclear chrome. The Apple and lock icons become one readable line: "On this device · Apple Intelligence".
  • Sources as an afterthought. The "S2" cards at the bottom become groups, rows or inline chips tied to the claim they support.

Shared-code note

Each mockup is one template cloned into both frames. Layout differences come from the available width (container queries here, ViewThatFits or size classes in SwiftUI), not from separate platform views. That maps to one AskMailView in PlainpostUI, with thin iOS sheet and macOS inspector hosts.
