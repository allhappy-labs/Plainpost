B · Query lens

No chat bubbles. Your question becomes a live, editable filter over the mail list, and the answer is the list itself, grouped and ready to act on.

OverviewABC
IdeaAsk is a mode of the mailbox, not a side chat. The question turns into filter chips (Unread · Inbox · All accounts) that you can remove or add to. The model only decides how to group and label the results.
Why it fitsMost mail questions are "find" or "clean up" tasks. Those end in a list plus a bulk action. Follow-ups refine the same lens, and the breadcrumb lets you step back.
TryTap a section's circle to select it and the action bar counts up. On Mac, the wide layout adds a side panel with the summary and shortcuts. Same component, container-query layout.
